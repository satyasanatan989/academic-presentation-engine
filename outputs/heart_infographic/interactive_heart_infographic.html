The Brain: High-Priority Uninterrupted Perfusion

~750 mL/min (15% CO)

The human brain accounts for just 2% of total body mass, yet demands 15% of the heart's total cardiac output and 20% of its resting oxygen delivery. Neurons operate without glycogen reserves; interruption of cardiac output causes loss of consciousness in 6-8 seconds and irreversible ischemic necrosis within 4-5 minutes.

Primary Conduit: Carotid Arteries & Circle of Willis
Autoregulation Zone: MAP 60 - 160 mmHg
Critical Vulnerability: Ischemic Stroke & Syncope

5 Fundamental Uses of the Heart Organ

1. Hydrostatic Pressure Engine

Maintains continuous Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP >65 mmHg) powering microvascular filtration through billions of capillary networks.

2. Respiratory Gas Transporter

Propels 100% of body blood volume through pulmonary alveoli every 60s, carrying 1,000 mL O2/min to periphery and exhausting volatile CO2.

3. Metabolic & Waste Highway

Distributes absorbed nutrients (glucose, fatty acids, amino acids) and continuously shunts metabolic toxins (urea, creatinine) to kidneys & liver.

4. Endocrine Fluid Sensor

Atrial and ventricular myocytes sense volume stretch and release ANP & BNP to trigger renal natriuresis, diuresis, and systemic vasodilation.

5. Thermal & Immune Defense Distributor

Shunts heated core blood to cutaneous vessels for evaporative heat dissipation, and rapidly mobilizes leukocytes, platelets, and antibodies to injury sites within seconds.
